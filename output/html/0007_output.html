About me

I'm a founding product engineer who loves the messy early stage of a product, when the idea is still ambiguous and someone needs to figure out how to actually build it. I've spent the last 5+ years doing exactly that across B2B SaaS and AI-powered products.

These days I'm leading product engineering at Kay.ai, where I own the frontend architecture and UX for an AI Copilot that automates insurance workflows for brokers. Before that, I co-founded a couple of startups, built an LLMOps platform, and scaled systems to 13M+ users.

I studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Jodhpur with a minor in Entrepreneurship, and I've also written 50+ technical blogs on Medium that have reached more than 400K readers. I'm happiest working across the whole stack, from React and TypeScript to Rails, Go, and applied LLMs.
