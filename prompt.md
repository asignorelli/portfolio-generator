## Prompt
This is used for prompting the LLM to generate a portfolio from our example resumes:

Go through the resume attached and generate a portfolio according to a style that fits the person based on their resume. For the outputs: Give all the files for the portfolio (HTML, CSS, JS).  

While generating the portfolio: include their experiences, projects, education, skills and other details as seen in the resume. Also add a short professional summary that describes them in the portfolio. Keep the language casual but professional; do not use resume-like language focused solely on metrics or bullet points. Use complete sentences that sound inviting and are conversational. If there are any links that cannot be embedded, write <INSERT LINK FOR ___ HERE> where the blank can be their LinkedIn/GitHub...etc. 

Do not make up any facts, experiences, or metrics for the portfolio. Stick strictly to the information provided in the resume.
